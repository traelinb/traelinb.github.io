I am a second-year undergraduate studying **Data Science and Big Data Technology** at **Tongji University**, in Guohao College.

My current research work focuses on **tabular data synthesis**. I reproduce generative baselines, build reusable experiment pipelines, and evaluate how well synthetic data preserves the statistical properties of real data.

### Research interests

- Generative modeling for tabular data
- Synthetic data evaluation
- Reproducible machine learning experiments

### Current work

I am contributing to the experimental work of a tabular data synthesis project. My work includes data preparation, baseline reproduction, seeded sampling, and evaluation. I am currently developing a reusable SDV pipeline for datasets with mixed numerical and categorical features.
